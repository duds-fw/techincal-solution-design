# Post-Implementation Review (PIR)

> Enterprise template for reviewing implementation outcomes after production deployment.

---

# 1. Purpose

## Description

The Post-Implementation Review (PIR) captures the outcomes, lessons learned, and improvement opportunities after a release has been deployed to production.

The PIR is not a blame exercise. It is a structured reflection to improve future delivery, reduce recurring issues, and strengthen the organization's engineering practices.

---

# 2. Objectives

This document aims to:

- Evaluate release outcomes against objectives.
- Capture lessons learned.
- Identify improvements for future releases.
- Document incidents and their root causes.
- Strengthen engineering practices.
- Support continuous improvement.

---

# 3. When to Conduct

Conduct the PIR:

- 1-2 weeks after production deployment (after hypercare).
- After a major incident related to a release.
- After a rollback.
- At the end of a project.
